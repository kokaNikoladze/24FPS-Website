A focused 4K/24 Apple Log 2 camera and paired-device remote for iPhone and iPad.

Support

For help, feedback or privacy questions, contact contact@koka.one. Include your device model, iOS version and a short description. Please do not send private footage unless needed and you choose to share it.

Camera and remote

Camera mode requires iOS 26 and compatible 4K/24 Apple Log 2 camera hardware. Remote mode requires iOS 18 or later. Devices without supported camera hardware can be used as remotes.

Pairing

Open the remote’s QR button. Point the idle 24FPS camera at the code and tap Pair. Scanning a remembered remote offers Unpair. Keep Wi-Fi enabled on both devices. Internet access is not required for monitoring.

Color preview

Built-in color transforms are provided for monitoring and playback. Preview adjustments do not alter original recordings.

Recordings

Allow Photos access to save and browse clips. If saving fails, keep the app installed to preserve any recording retained in its storage. Email support before deleting the app.
